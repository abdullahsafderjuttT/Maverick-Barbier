Our Story

Precision is the whole point.

Maverick Barbier opened on Rue St-Zotique with one idea: a haircut should be treated like a craft, not a transaction. Every chair is run by a barber who trained for years to get a single line right.

We keep the shop small on purpose — five barbers, one room, no rush. You'll leave knowing exactly who cut your hair, and you'll come back for the same chair next time.
